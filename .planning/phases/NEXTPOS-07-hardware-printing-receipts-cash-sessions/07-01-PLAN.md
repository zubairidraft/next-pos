# Phase 7 Plan 1: ESC/POS Thermal Receipt Engine & 1-Bit Urdu Raster Bitmaps

**Plan ID:** `07-01`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-07-hardware-printing-receipts-cash-sessions`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement `crates/pos-core/src/hardware/escpos.rs`:
   - Formatter for 58mm and 80mm receipt rolls.
   - Text formatting: store header, bilingual items, prices, tax summary, and footer greetings.
   - 1-bit monochrome raster bitmap converter (`GS v 0`) for rendering Urdu Nastaliq store and item names directly onto thermal paper.
   - Barcode and QR code ESC/POS commands (e.g. invoice tracking QR).
   - Paper cut commands (`GS V 66 0`).
2. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/receipt_escpos_tests.rs`.
