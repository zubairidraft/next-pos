1. Update `PrinterSettingsModal.tsx`:

   - Store selected printer name and roll width (58mm/80mm) in `localStorage`.

   - Maintain browser `window.print()` as secondary A4/PDF fallback.
3. Update POS `CheckoutView.tsx`:
   - Auto-trigger cash drawer kick on cash payment completion.
   - 1-click silent receipt print on checkout completion.
