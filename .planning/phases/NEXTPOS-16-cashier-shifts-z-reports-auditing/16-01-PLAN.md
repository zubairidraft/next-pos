# Phase 16 Plan 1: Cashier Shifts, Petty Cash & Z-Report Auditing

**Plan ID:** `16-01`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-16-cashier-shifts-z-reports-auditing`  
**Wave:** 1  
**Target:** `apps/desktop/src-tauri`, `ShiftCloseModal.tsx`, `CheckoutView.tsx`, `api.ts`  

## Objectives
1. Connect `cash_sessions` SQLite persistence in `ShiftRepo`:
   - `open_shift(opening_float_paisa)`: Creates or restores active cash session.
   - `get_active_shift()`: Queries open shift for active cashier.
   - `record_cash_drop(amount_paisa, reason)`: Records petty cash expense or safe drops.
   - `close_shift(closing_counted_cash_paisa)`: Audits physical counted drawer cash against system expected balance, calculates variance, and compiles immutable Z-Report.
2. Connect `ShiftCloseModal.tsx`:
   - Physical Pakistani currency denomination counter (`n5000`, `n1000`, `n500`, `n100`, `n50`, `n20`, `n10`, `coins`).
   - Hardware print Z-Report directly to ESC/POS thermal printer.
3. Wire `CheckoutView.tsx`:
   - Auto-retrieves or establishes active cashier shift session on POS terminal launch.
