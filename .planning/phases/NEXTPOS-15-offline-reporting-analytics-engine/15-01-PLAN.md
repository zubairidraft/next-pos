# Phase 15 Plan 1: Offline Reporting & Analytics Engine

**Plan ID:** `15-01`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-15-offline-reporting-analytics-engine`  
**Wave:** 1  
**Target:** `apps/desktop/src-tauri`, `apps/desktop/src/components/ReportsView.tsx`, `api.ts`  

## Objectives
1. Expose high-performance SQLite analytics queries from `pos-core`:
   - `get_reports_summary(start_date, end_date)`: Gross/Net sales, COGS, gross margins, average ticket, payment breakdown.
   - `get_category_performance(start_date, end_date)`: Department revenue, COGS, profit and margins.
   - `get_top_products(start_date, end_date, limit)`: Best-selling SKUs by revenue and velocity.
   - `get_inventory_valuation()`: Total cost vs. retail valuation and unrealized profit.
   - `get_low_stock_report()`: Reorder point violations and suggested replenishment orders.
2. Connect `ReportsView.tsx` with date range bounds ("today", "yesterday", "week", "month", "year") and live vs. demo preview status indicators.
3. Provide one-click multi-format CSV exports for accounting.
