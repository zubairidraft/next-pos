# Phase 12 Plan 2: Hardware Certification Suite for ESC/POS & Scanners

**Plan ID:** `12-02`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-12-pilot-hardening-ga-launch`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement hardware certification and diagnostic profiles in `crates/pos-core/src/hardware/certified.rs`:
   - Printer profile presets: Standard ESC/POS, Epson TM series, Xprinter, Rongta, Black Copper.
   - Adjust codepage mappings, raster line pacing, and cash drawer pulse widths per manufacturer preset.
   - Scanner preamble/postamble normalization (handling Enter, Tab, CR/LF suffixes consistently across different scanner hardware).
2. Diagnostic print test page generating bilingual alignment grid, 1-bit Nastaliq typography sample, 1D barcode test, and QR code scan test.
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/hardware_compatibility_tests.rs`.
