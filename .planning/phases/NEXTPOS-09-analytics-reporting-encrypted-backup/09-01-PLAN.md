# Phase 9 Plan 1: Offline Reporting & Financial Intelligence Engine

**Plan ID:** `09-01`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-09-analytics-reporting-encrypted-backup`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement analytics repository in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/analytics.rs`:
   - Daily/Period Sales Summary: Total revenue, item count, discounts, tax, net sales in `Paisa(i64)`.
   - Category and Department Performance: Revenue and gross margin percentages.
   - Profit & Loss Report: Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit (`Revenue - COGS`).
   - Inventory Valuation: Total stock on hand valued at moving-average cost.
   - Cashier Performance & Tender Breakdown: Cash, Card, Khata Credit, Bank Transfer.
2. Fast aggregated SQL queries utilizing covering indexes without table scans.
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/analytics_reports_tests.rs`.
