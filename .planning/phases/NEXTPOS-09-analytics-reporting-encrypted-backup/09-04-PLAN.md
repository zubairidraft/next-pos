# Phase 9 Plan 4: Reports Dashboard & Backup/Restore Desktop UI

**Plan ID:** `09-04`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-09-analytics-reporting-encrypted-backup`  
**Target:** `apps/desktop`  

## Objectives
1. Implement Reports UI in `apps/desktop`:
   - `ReportsView.tsx`: Date range picker (Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, Custom).
   - KPI metrics: Gross Sales, Net Profit, Average Ticket Size, Items Sold.
   - Breakdown charts: Sales by Category, Top 10 Fast-Selling Products, Payment Methods.
   - Export reports to CSV and print directly to thermal receipt (80mm summary).
2. Backup & Restore UI:
   - `BackupView.tsx`: Displays last automatic snapshot status with green/amber health badge.
   - "Backup to USB / File" button with optional password prompt.
   - "Restore from Backup" modal with pre-restore inspection warnings and confirmation safeguard.
