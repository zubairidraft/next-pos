# Phase 13 Plan 4: Connect Customers, KhataView, and Purchasing to SQLite

**Plan ID:** `13-04`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-13-core-data-wiring-sqlite-persistence`  
**Wave:** 3  
**Target:** `apps/desktop/src/components/CheckoutView.tsx`, `KhataView.tsx`, & `PurchasingView.tsx`  

## Objectives
1. Connect inline customer creation in `CheckoutView.tsx` to `api.createParty({ party_type: "customer", ... })`.
2. Connect `KhataView.tsx` to `api.listKhataParties("customer")` and `api.listKhataParties("vendor")`.
3. Allow recording Khata payments/collections that update running balance in SQLite.
4. Connect `PurchasingView.tsx` vendor list to `api.listKhataParties("vendor")` and creation to `api.createParty({ party_type: "vendor", ... })`.
5. Verify customer and vendor persistence across sessions.
