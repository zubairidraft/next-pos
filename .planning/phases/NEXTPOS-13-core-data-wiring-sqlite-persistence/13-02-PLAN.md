# Phase 13 Plan 2: Connect CatalogView and ProductFormModal to SQLite

**Plan ID:** `13-02`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-13-core-data-wiring-sqlite-persistence`  
**Wave:** 2  
**Target:** `apps/desktop/src/components/CatalogView.tsx` & `ProductFormModal.tsx`  

## Objectives
1. Update `CatalogView.tsx` to load real products on mount via `api.listProductsCatalog()` or `api.searchProducts("")`.
2. Connect product creation in `ProductFormModal.tsx` to call `api.createProduct(...)`.
3. Support category, brand, and unit dropdowns dynamically populated from `api.listCategories()`, `api.listBrands()`, and `api.listUnits()`.
4. Persist retail price, wholesale price floor, barcode, and base unit directly to SQLite.
5. Provide automatic reload of the catalog table when a new product is saved.

- [ ] Task 1: Update `CatalogView.tsx` to fetch catalog items from Tauri IPC on mount.
- [ ] Task 2: Update `ProductFormModal.tsx` to populate category/brand/unit options from SQLite.
- [ ] Task 3: Wire `handleSaveProduct` to invoke `api.createProduct` and show success/error feedback.
- [ ] Task 4: Test in browser/Tauri and verify persistence across reload.
