## Objectives
1. Implement Tauri commands in `src-tauri/src/main.rs`:
   - `list_categories` & `create_category` (backed by `CategoryRepo`)
   - `list_brands` & `create_brand` (backed by `BrandRepo`)
   - `list_units` & `create_unit` (backed by `UnitRepo`)
   - `create_product` & `list_products_catalog` (backed by `CatalogRepo`)
   - `create_party` (backed by `PartyRepo`)

2. Register all new commands in `tauri::generate_handler!`.
3. Update `apps/desktop/src/lib/api.ts` with strongly-typed interfaces and methods for all new commands.
4. Verify compilation of both Rust backend (`cargo check`) and TypeScript frontend (`tsc`).
