# Phase 4 Plan 2: Purchase Invoices, Landed Cost & Weighted-Average Costing

**Plan ID:** `04-02`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-04-purchasing-costing-stock-ledger`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement `crates/pos-core/src/domain/purchase.rs`:
   - `PurchaseInvoice` and `PurchaseInvoiceLine` models.
   - Landed cost allocation algorithm (e.g., freight, unloading charges distributed across lines proportionally by gross amount).
   - Weighted-Average Cost (WAC) recalculation algorithm in pure integer arithmetic (`Paisa`, `Qty`).
2. Post purchase invoice transaction:
   - Inserts into `purchases` and `purchase_lines`.
   - Records positive inbound `stock_movements`.
   - Updates `inventory_balances` quantity and new average cost.
   - Increments document sequence `PUR-`.
3. Automated integration tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/purchase_costing_tests.rs`.
