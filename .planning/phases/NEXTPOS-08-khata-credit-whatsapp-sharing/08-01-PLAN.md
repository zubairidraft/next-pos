# Phase 8 Plan 1: Customer & Vendor Khata Ledgers & Running Balances

**Plan ID:** `08-01`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-08-khata-credit-whatsapp-sharing`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement Khata repository in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/khata.rs`:
   - Customer and vendor ledger entry structures with `transaction_type` (sale, purchase, payment_in, payment_out, adjustment, return).
   - Debit and credit amounts in `Paisa(i64)`.
   - Continuous running balance calculation with immutable historical ledger sequence.
2. Ledger querying:
   - Query ledger statement by customer/vendor ID and date range (`start_date` to `end_date`).
   - Calculate opening balance, total debits, total credits, and net closing balance.
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/khata_ledger_tests.rs`.
