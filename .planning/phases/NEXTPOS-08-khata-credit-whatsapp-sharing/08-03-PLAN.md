# Phase 8 Plan 3: Statement Generator & WhatsApp Deep-Link Builder

**Plan ID:** `08-03`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-08-khata-credit-whatsapp-sharing`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement statement formatter in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/khata.rs`:
   - Bilingual (English and Urdu) text summary and 80mm thermal printable statement format.
   - Summarizes opening balance, period invoices, payments received, and outstanding balance.
2. WhatsApp deep-link generation:
   - Construct phone-formatted WhatsApp URL (`https://wa.me/92300xxxxxxx?text=...`) without external API dependencies.
   - URL-encode friendly Urdu and English reminder messages including store name, date, invoice summary, and total pending amount.
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/khata_statement_tests.rs`.
