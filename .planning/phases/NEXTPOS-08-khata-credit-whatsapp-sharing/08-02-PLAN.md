# Phase 8 Plan 2: Automated FIFO Payment Allocation & Credit Limits

**Plan ID:** `08-02`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-08-khata-credit-whatsapp-sharing`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement payment allocation in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/khata.rs`:
   - FIFO allocation algorithm: matches incoming customer payment against oldest unpaid invoices first.
   - Updates invoice payment status (`unpaid`, `partially_paid`, `paid`) and remaining balance.
2. Credit limit enforcement:
   - Check customer credit balance + cart total against customer `credit_limit`.
   - Provide override capability with manager PIN authentication when credit ceiling is breached.
3. Aging analysis:
   - Calculate receivable aging buckets (Current, 30 days, 60 days, 90+ days).
4. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/khata_allocation_tests.rs`.
