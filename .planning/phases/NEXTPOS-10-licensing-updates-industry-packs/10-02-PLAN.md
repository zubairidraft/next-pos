# Phase 10 Plan 2: Grocery Pack — Scale Barcode Parser & Tare Deduction

**Plan ID:** `10-02`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-10-licensing-updates-industry-packs`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement scale barcode parser in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/grocery.rs`:
   - Detect prefixes `20` through `29` (Standard in-store weighing scales).
   - Configurable parser formats:
     - Format A: `20` + `5-digit SKU` + `5-digit Price (Paisa)` + `Check digit`
     - Format B: `21` + `5-digit SKU` + `5-digit Weight (Grams)` + `Check digit`
   - Calculate quantity or price automatically and match to master catalog product.
2. Weight scale hardware integration:
   - Optional serial COM port interface for electronic counter scales (continuous tare/gross reading).
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/grocery_scale_tests.rs`.
