# Phase 10 Plan 3: Electronics Pack (IMEI Tracking) & Apparel Variants

**Plan ID:** `10-03`  
**Phase:** `NEXTPOS-10-licensing-updates-industry-packs`  
**Target:** `crates/pos-core`  

## Objectives
1. Implement serial & IMEI tracking in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/electronics.rs`:
   - `product_serials` table: `id`, `product_id`, `serial_number`, `status` (in_stock, sold, returned, defective), `purchase_line_id`, `sale_line_id`, `warranty_months`.
   - Compulsory prompt for serial/IMEI scan on purchase receipt and sale checkout.
   - Validation against duplicate active serials in stock.
2. Implement apparel matrix variants in `crates/pos-core/src/domain/apparel.rs`:
   - Variant generator for size x color matrices (e.g. Small/Medium/Large x Blue/Black).
   - Generates distinct SKUs and barcodes tied to parent style.
3. Tests in `crates/pos-core/tests/electronics_serial_tests.rs`.
